Company + Customer Estimate — Example

Company estimate holds detailed items under Line #1, #2… (QuickBooks sync source). Customer estimate shows one bundle per line: stacked descriptions and summed totals. Customer edits stick until that company line is edited again.

How the sync works
  1. Create / edit items under Line #1, #2… on the company estimate (QB sync stays on these detailed rows).
  2. Customer estimate gets one row per line: descriptions stacked, total = sum of that line’s company items.
  3. If you edit the customer line, it sticks until someone edits that same line on the company estimate — then it rebuilds.
